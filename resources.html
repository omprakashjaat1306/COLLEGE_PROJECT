You Already Use the Cloud. Every Day.

Here are some examples you've probably experienced yourself

🎥

Netflix

Netflix runs almost entirely on AWS. When you hit play, that video streams from cloud servers — not your laptop. AWS handles storage, encoding, and delivery for millions of users at once.

AWSStreamingCDN
🎵

Spotify

Spotify uses Google Cloud to store and stream 100M+ songs. The cloud runs those personalized playlists (Discover Weekly) and makes sure the song doesn't buffer mid-chorus.

GCPMusicBig Data
📷

Google Photos

Every photo you backup goes to Google Cloud servers. That "free up space" button? It deletes local copies. Your photos stay safe in the cloud with Google's AI organizing them.

GCPStorageAI
📩

Gmail / Outlook

Ever noticed you can log into your email from any device? That's because your emails don't live on your phone — they live in the cloud (Gmail on GCP, Outlook on Azure).

AWSAzureGCP
💳

Amazon

Amazon runs its entire store on AWS. Every search, payment, and "Customers also bought" is cloud-powered. During Prime Day, it scales automatically to handle the traffic surge.

AWSE-CommerceScaling
🏢

Airbnb

Airbnb uses AWS to handle millions of bookings. The cloud lets them scale up during holiday travel season and scale down when things are quiet — paying only for what they use.

AWSTravelServerless

The Big Three Cloud Platforms

Pick one. Don't try to learn all three at once. I'd recommend starting with AWS if you're not sure.

AWS

Amazon Web Services

Market leader with 200+ services. Best for beginners — the free tier is generous and there's a tutorial for everything.

  • Free Tier: 12 months + always free stuff
  • Best For: Everyone, honestly. Especially beginners.
  • Key Services: EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS
  • Certification: AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)
Free Tier ↗ Learn AWS ↗
Azure

Microsoft Azure

Great if your college or company already uses Microsoft stuff. Integrates well with Office 365 and Active Directory.

  • Free Tier: 12 months + $200 credit for 30 days
  • Best For: .NET devs, enterprise folks, hybrid cloud
  • Key Services: VMs, Azure Functions, SQL Database
  • Certification: AZ-900 Azure Fundamentals
Free Tier ↗ Learn Azure ↗
GCP

Google Cloud Platform

Best if you're into data stuff, machine learning, or containers. Google basically invented Kubernetes.

  • Free Tier: $300 credit for 90 days + always free
  • Best For: Data science, ML, containers, startups
  • Key Services: BigQuery, Cloud Run, GKE, Compute Engine
  • Certification: Google Cloud Digital Leader
Free Tier ↗ Learn GCP ↗

Step-by-Step Learning Path

This is exactly what I did. It worked for me, it'll probably work for you too.

1

Understand Basic Internet Stuff

You don't need to be a networking expert, but know what IP addresses, DNS, and servers are. Watch a 20-minute YouTube video and you're good.

YouTubeKhan AcademyFree
2

Pick One Platform (Just One!)

AWS, Azure, or GCP. Pick one and stick with it. I picked AWS because it has the most free content. Don't try to learn all three at once — I made that mistake, it doesn't work.

AWSAzureGCP
3

Free YouTube Courses Are Your Friend

Don't pay for courses yet. freeCodeCamp, AWS Official, and a hundred other channels have amazing free content. Search "AWS beginners full course" and start watching.

freeCodeCampYouTubeFree
4

Sign Up for Free Tier (It's Actually Free)

AWS gives you 12 months free. Azure gives $200 credit. GCP gives $300. Sign up, explore the dashboard, launch a virtual machine. Break things — it's free to experiment.

AWS FreeAzure FreeGCP Free
5

Build Something Tiny

Host a "Hello World" website on S3. Deploy a simple app on EC2. Set up a database and connect it to something. You'll learn more from one real project than 10 hours of videos.

EC2S3VMs
6

Get Certified (It's Easier Than You Think)

Beginner certs (AWS Cloud Practitioner, AZ-900, GCP Digital Leader) are doable in 2-4 weeks. They look great on resumes and actually teach you the fundamentals properly.

AWS CPAZ-900GCP CDL
