So, What's This All About?

A college project that turned into something bigger — making cloud computing less scary for everyone

Why I Made This

Honestly? I was confused. Every article about cloud computing used words like "scalability," "virtualization," and "elasticity" like everyone just knew what those meant. I didn't. So I decided to build something that explains it the way I wish someone had explained it to me — in plain language, with real examples, and without the corporate nonsense.

CloudGuide started as a semester project at SKIT Jaipur. I had to pick a topic, make a website, and present it. But the more I dug into cloud computing, the more I realized how many people are in the same boat — they use the cloud every day (Netflix, Google Drive, Spotify) but have no idea how any of it works. So I kept building.

What You'll Find Here

  • Real-talk explanations of cloud concepts (no jargon, I promise)
  • A breakdown of IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS with actual examples
  • Where to start learning AWS, Azure, or GCP without burning money
  • Everyday examples of cloud computing you've probably used today
  • Resources and certification paths that actually make sense for beginners
