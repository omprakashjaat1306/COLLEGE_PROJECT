Cloud Service Models

Three ways to use the cloud — pick whatever makes sense for what you're trying to build

How Much Control Do You Want?

That's really what it comes down to. More control = more responsibility. Less control = less to manage.

🏙

IaaS

Infrastructure as a Service — you get virtual computers, storage, and networking. Basically a computer in the cloud that you control completely.

  • Virtual machines (rent a computer)
  • Block & object storage (save files)
  • Virtual networking (connect things)
  • Load balancers (handle traffic)
  • Full root access (do whatever you want)
Talk to Us
💻

PaaS

Platform as a Service — just upload your code and the platform handles the rest. No worrying about servers or operating systems.

  • App hosting (deploy without setup)
  • Database management (they handle backups)
  • CI/CD pipelines (auto-deploy)
  • Auto-scaling (handles traffic spikes)
  • Built-in security (one less thing to worry about)
Talk to Us
🎮

SaaS

Software as a Service — just use the app. No setup, no maintenance, no IT team needed. Open a browser and you're done.

  • Email & collaboration (Gmail, Office 365)
  • CRM & analytics (Salesforce, Google Analytics)
  • File storage & sharing (Google Drive, Dropbox)
  • Automatic updates (always the latest version)
  • 24/7 support (someone else fixes problems)
